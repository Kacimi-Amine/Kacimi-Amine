## 👋 About Me

Software Engineer building **AI systems that ship** — autonomous agents, RAG pipelines and business process automation — on top of **5+ years of production software engineering**.

- 🤖 **Building** AI agents and agentic workflows, RAG knowledge bases, and **AI-powered financial automation** (invoice & receipt extraction, bank statement analysis, transaction reconciliation).
- 📏 I care about **evaluating** LLM systems, not just building them — golden sets, LLM-as-a-judge, regression gates before anything reaches production.
- 🏗️ **Team Leader** on an AdTech SaaS platform — winner of the **IAB MIXX Awards 2024 Gold**, Best International Marketing Tool.
- ⚙️ Comfortable across the whole chain: Spring Boot / Angular back & front ends, APIs, CI/CD, Docker, cloud.
- 💬 Ask me about **AI agents, RAG, LLM evaluation, prompt engineering** — or Spring Boot & Angular.
- 📫 Reach me at **kacimi.aminee@gmail.com** or on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amine-kacimi).
